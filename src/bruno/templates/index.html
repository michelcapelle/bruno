About Bruno

Bruno is an exploratory tool to trace how ideas and concepts (as topics) appear across influential military strategic thinkers. It combines processed Wikipedia texts, (Wikipedia) DBpedia metadata, and Ollama LLM prompt analyses to generate topics and approximate which thinkers are associated with them.

  1. ThinkersAdd thinkers and their associated texts to generate Ollama LLM analyses on their contributions to military strategy.
  2. TopicsGenerated topics using the BERTopic algorithm, their keywords and statistically assigned thinkers.
  3. VisualizationsDiscover how topics and thinkers relate to each other.
